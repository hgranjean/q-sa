# Quality Smart Assistant - Product Positioning

## Product Intent
Quality Smart Assistant is a Quality Management assistant that helps teams plan assessments, execute responses, and track completion outcomes.

## Core Product Pillars
1. Quality Assessment Design
2. Assignment and Execution
3. Completion and Performance Visibility

## Naming Alignment
- Preferred product name: Quality Smart Assistant
- Avoid generic "Survey" naming in user-facing artifacts unless required for backward compatibility.
- For modern services, use `QualitySmartAssistant.*` project naming.

## Architecture Direction
- Contract-first APIs (OpenAPI)
- .NET 8 Minimal APIs for service delivery
- Incremental replacement of legacy modules, prioritizing quality-management workflows
